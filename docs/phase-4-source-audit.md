## Entry association decisions
- Roster `Male` / `Female` become `Men` / `Women` query values. Original roster values and pages remain in immutable evidence. `Women` or `Men` filtering uses the applicable roster entry; `Mixed` selects a source mixed-gender event and includes all its member rows. Mixed is an overlapping event scope, so its option count is not added to Men/Women totals.
- Gender suffixes are separated from the exact reviewed division labels. `Junior A-B-Senior` and `Combined` remain combined divisions; they are not split into guessed age categories. `Group 1 Master` and `Group 2 Master` are actual roster divisions. `Group A` / `Group B` are retained separately as competition groups.
- Short-track roster pages 4–5 explicitly list men and women in each Master division. RESULTS pages 83–84 show men and women racing together under the same Master headings. These are mixed-gender source events, even when a filtered subset happens to contain only one gender. Original places and qualifications remain unchanged.
- Long-track bib 370 has `Junior A-B-Senior Women` on roster page 3 and `Master Women` on page 4. RESULTS associate races 401, 2001, 3301 and 4601 with Master, and race 5801 with Junior A-B-Senior. The exact heading selects the applicable roster entry. Neither entry is copied onto all five results.
- Source event identity remains the existing heading-derived ID. Reused printed event numbers, rounds, distances, competition groups and mixed scopes do not collapse into a shared ranking. A result appears once in each applicable filtered response.
- Where exactly one roster entry cannot be resolved under the reviewed source heading, `entry_id` stays null and result dimensions stay Unknown. The original row is retained. Duplicate eligible entries do not result in duplicate rows or arbitrary assignment.
